Engineering expertise: project survey

Once a quarter, tell us which engineering expertise your team needs next quarter, and how well your team covers it. About ten minutes. We never ask about named people.

Why we ask: the answers show the discipline where engineering is short or has more than it needs, where to grow people, and who could help whom. Marking a row "over" starts a conversation with you. Nobody is moved without one.

How to answer
  • Sign in with Google, then pick your team.
  • Pick the areas your team might need next quarter. Your project types suggest some. If in doubt, pick it.
  • Related areas share one set of questions, so picking more areas rarely means more to answer.
  • People needed is a headcount: count heads, not time. A part-time person counts as one. The same person can count in several areas.
  • Covered asks how your team stands today. Critical means the gap blocks delivery or breaches an obligation.
  • Think about the whole next quarter. Count expertise you'll need for part of it, or only later in it. Use the comment to say so if it matters.
