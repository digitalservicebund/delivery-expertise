## About this survey

Every quarter, each team tells us what engineering work it needs and how the quarter looks with the people it has. It takes about ten minutes. We never ask about named people.

The answers show where the discipline is short or has more than it needs, where to grow people, and who could help whom. Marking a row "over" is how other teams get help, and how yours gets help next time. It starts a conversation with you. Nobody is moved without one.

### How to answer

**Answer only the areas your team needs.** Leave everything else blank.

**Grid: how many people need to work this way?** Count people, not time. The same person can count in several areas. Pick the closest of 1, 2, 3 or 5.

**Also from…** Some areas have a team or community you can call on: the platform team for DevOps, CI/CD and infrastructure, the legal and IT security team for compliance, and communities of practice for most others. Tick the last column where you expect to draw on them. It is in addition to the people your team needs, not instead of them, so a row can have both a headcount and a tick.

| Working method | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Operative** | Implementing, applying standard methods, finding solutions, executing |
| **Conceptual** | Planning, coordinating, shaping and guiding; adapting methods to the situation |
| **Strategic** | Technically innovative and entrepreneurial with limited resources; creating guidelines, leading, looking ahead |
| **Comprehensive** | Strategic across the wider ecosystem, including trends and developments beyond the project |

**Critical, under, adequate, over:** for each working method you need, is it covered on your team today? Pick one per row. Critical means the gap blocks delivery or breaches a legal or security obligation.
