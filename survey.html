Engineering expertise: project survey

Every quarter, each team tells us what engineering work it needs and how the quarter looks with the people it has. It takes about ten minutes. We never ask about named people.

The answers show where the discipline is short or has more than it needs, where to grow people, and who could help whom. Marking a row "over" is how other teams get help, and how yours gets help next time. It starts a conversation with you. Nobody is moved without one.

How to answer

Answer only the areas your team needs. Leave everything else on "Not needed".

How many people need to work this way? Count people, not time. The same person can count in several areas. Pick the closest of 1, 2, 3 or 5.

Critical, under, adequate, over: for each working method you need, is it covered on your team today? Critical means the gap blocks delivery or breaches an obligation.

Also from… Tick where you expect to draw on a team or community: the platform team, the legal and IT security team, or a community of practice. It is in addition to the people your team needs, not instead of them.

Operative

Strategic

Comprehensive
